# Visual asset provenance

The six editorial scenes were generated for the Legacy Search website using OpenAI ImageGen on 25 November 2026. They illustrate business ownership and industrial activity. The people, premises and assets pictured do not identify Legacy Search staff, clients or mandates.

| Local image | Subject |
| --- | --- |
| `public/assets/images/family.webp` | Two generations working at a textile loom |
| `public/assets/images/industry.webp` | Molten metal pouring in a foundry |
| `public/assets/images/strategy.webp` | Production machinery beneath a sawtooth roof |
| `public/assets/images/operator.webp` | An operator at a precision lathe |
| `public/assets/images/capital.webp` | Structural columns surrounding an institutional courtyard |
| `public/assets/images/border.webp` | A container vessel at a commercial quay |

The 1200 × 630 social image in `public/assets/images/share.jpg` combines the foundry illustration with the supplied brand copy. The six local WebP images are 512 × 512 pixels, with a combined size below 285 KB.

The Legacy Search logo supplied with the brief is company brand material. The representative operator in the editorial image should never be used as the founder's photograph.

The locally hosted Inter variable font is from the Inter project’s published web font distribution. Instrument Serif regular and italic are from Google Fonts. Each font is distributed with its SIL Open Font License in `public/assets/fonts/`.
